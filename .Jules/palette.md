## 2025-05-15 - [Accessibility & Form Labels]
**Learning:** Always associate form labels with inputs using `htmlFor` and `id` to improve screen reader accessibility. Adding `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"` to modals is essential for correct screen reader behavior.
**Action:** Use a consistent naming convention for form IDs (e.g., `prefix-fieldname`) and ensure all interactive elements have appropriate ARIA labels or descriptions.
